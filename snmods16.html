SharedPreferences

SharedPreferences is how Android apps store persistent data - like settings, scores, coins, and user data. It's like a small database that survives even after the app closes.

💾 Why SharedPreferences matters for modding:
Many apps store VIP status, coins, levels, unlocked items, and settings in SharedPreferences. If you can modify the data stored here, you can unlock anything without modifying the code!

🔥 Modding Example: Unlimited Coins via Preferences
Find where the app saves your coins.

Original: putInt("coins", v0)
Modified: const/16 v0, 0x2710 # 10000 coins
putInt("coins", v0)

Now when the app saves coins, it saves 10000 coins instead of the normal amount! ✅

💡 Pro Tip:
Look for preferences with obvious names:
🔹 "coins", "score", "level" → Game data
🔹 "is_vip", "premium", "unlocked" → Premium features
🔹 "settings", "config" → App settings

Sometimes you can simply edit the preferences file directly on your device!

⚡ Quick Mod:
Find getInt("coins") and change the method to always return a huge value.

Replace the call with:

Now no matter what's saved, you'll always have 10000 coins! 🚀
